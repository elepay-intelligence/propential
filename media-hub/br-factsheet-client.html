Propential
Client fact sheet
Provided by your mortgage broker

A second-mortgage loan for the work that lifts what your property is worth.

Propential is secured against your property and sits behind your existing home loan. It funds the project now so the revaluation, and the refinance that follows, happens on the finished result.

Loan amount$5,000 to $175,000
TermUp to 7 years ($5,000 to $50,000). Up to 10 years ($50,001 - $175,000). Repay in full at any time.
Early exitNo early-exit fees.
SecuritySecured by caveat, second mortgage in NT and QLD.
Maximum LVR95% combined for owner-occupiers, 90% combined for investors
RepaymentsRegular principal and interest, direct debit
AssessmentOn Propential's own lending criteria, independent of your current lender

What it can fund

  • Renovations, extensions and structural work
  • Kitchens, bathrooms and finishing trades
  • Granny flats, studios and secondary dwellings
  • Subdivision costs, site works and services
  • Landscaping, pools, decking and driveways
  • Solar, batteries and energy upgrades

Why people use it

  • The rate you already have stays put. No refinancing the whole loan to release equity.
  • No waiting on a top-up. Assessed separately from your existing lender.
  • Priced for a short life. No penalty for repaying it as soon as the valuation lands.
How it works
1

Bridge

Propential funds the project behind your existing loan.

2

Uplift

Work completes and the property is revalued.

3

Refinance out

Your broker refinances at the new value and Propential is repaid.

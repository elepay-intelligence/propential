When to raise it

  • The customer asks to stage the work
  • The customer asks you to cut the scope to hit a budget
  • The quote goes quiet after a week
  • At any stage of the works, they advise they may need postpone or reduce costs
  • Any job where the work will lift what the property is worth

What to say

  • “We have a dedicated support person who can help you with understanding the product and with your application.”
  • “Some customers fund work like this against the property rather than out of savings.”
  • “It sits behind your existing home loan. That loan and rate don't change.”
  • “Here's the information. Have a look and decide for yourself.”
  • “I can't advise you on finance, but Propential can answer questions directly.”

How it runs

01Quote as normal. Price the full job. Do not shrink the scope in advance.
02Attach the customer fact sheet or the flyer to the quote email.
03Customer checks online using the website link or QR code. Two minutes, no credit check.
04Customer applies directly with Propential. You are not part of the application.
05Funds settle and the customer confirms the job. You invoice the customer as usual.

The line your team must not cross:
 Do not tell a customer a loan is suitable for them, that they will be approved, or how much to borrow. Do not discuss their income, debts or credit history. Do not fill in an application or handle their documents. Do not make a quote conditional on using Propential.

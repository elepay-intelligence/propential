Clients to look for

  • Equity-rich, servicing-tight owners who want to renovate before selling or refinancing
  • Clients on a rate or fixed term they do not want to break
  • Top-up declined, or the existing lender is too slow
  • Construction loan shortfall: landscaping, driveways, fencing, finishing trades
  • Granny flat, studio or subdivision costs ahead of a revaluation
  • Investors preparing a property for market or for a higher rental assessment

Talking to the client

  • “We are not touching your current loan.”
  • “This funds the work; we refinance on the finished value.”
  • “There is no penalty for paying it out early.”

Applicant support

  • An on-demand BDM and Customer Success team support your applicants.

The process

01Scope. Confirm the works, the cost and the expected end value with the client.
02Refer. Refer clients to the Propential website. The client applies online.
03Assess. Propential assesses on its own criteria and settles secured by caveat (2nd mortgage in QLD and NT).
04Works complete. Client draws down, project finishes.
05Revalue and refinance. You write the refinance at the new valuation; Propential is repaid at settlement.

Why it protects your book

  • The first mortgage is untouched: no break costs, no trail disturbance, no re-pricing conversation.
  • The refinance stays yours. The client comes back to you at the uplift.
  • No exit fee means the exit can be timed around your refinance, not around a penalty.

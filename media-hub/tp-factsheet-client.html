Property-secured finance

Fund the work against the property you already own.

Propential is a loan secured against your property. It sits behind your existing home loan, so the loan and the rate you already have stay exactly as they are. You use it to get the work done now instead of staging it over years.

Loan amount$5,000 to $175,000
TermUp to 7 years ($5,000 to $50,000). Up to 10 years ($50,001 - $175,000). Repay in full at any time.
Early exitNo early exit fees. Pay it out early at no extra cost.
SecuritySecured by caveat, second mortgage in NT and QLD.
Maximum LVR95% combined for owner-occupiers, 90% combined for investors
RepaymentsRegular principal and interest, direct debit
AssessmentOn Propential's own lending criteria, independent of your current lender

What it can pay for

  • Landscaping, decking, paving and driveways
  • Pools, spas, fencing and retaining walls
  • Painting, roofing, windows and repairs
  • Kitchens, bathrooms and internal renovation
  • Solar, batteries and energy upgrades
  • Granny flats, studios and outbuildings

Why people use it

  • Your current loan is untouched. No refinancing the whole mortgage to release equity.
  • You do the work once. No staging the job across two or three seasons.
  • Refinance at the higher valuation once the work has lifted what the property is worth.

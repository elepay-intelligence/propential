What this hub is

A fixed set of approved Propential marketing material, prepared for trade and service partners. Everything has been through compliance review. You do not need to submit anything for approval, and you do not need a designer.

There are three kinds of item: customer-facing documents you send or hand over, an internal reference sheet for your own team, and digital banners for email, your website and social.

What this hub is not. It is not a template library. The wording, figures and the credit disclosure on every item are fixed. If something you need is missing, ask for it rather than making it.

When to raise it

The moment that works is when the customer has the number in front of them and is deciding whether to do all of the job, part of it, or none of it.

  • With the quote, as an attachment. Not instead of the quote.
  • When they ask to stage the work over two seasons or two financial years.
  • When they ask you to cut the scope to hit a budget.
  • At handover, for the next stage they have already talked about.

Keep it factual. “Some customers fund work like this against the property rather than out of savings. Here is the information. Have a look and decide for yourself.” Then stop and let the document do the rest.

Where to place banners

  • Email signature (600×160). It then goes out on every quote and every reply. Insert as an image, then set its hyperlink to https://propential.com.au.
  • Website footer (1200×250). Sitewide, so it appears on every page a customer reads.
  • Sidebar tower (300×600). Services and pricing pages. It carries a QR code so it still works in a screenshot.
  • Social (1080×1080, 1200×628, 1080×1920). Pair it with before-and-after work. Put the propential.com.au link in the caption or the story link sticker.

One placement beats five posts. If you only do one thing from this hub, put the signature banner in your email template today.

Rules and compliance

This is the part worth reading twice. You are referring customers to information. You are not arranging finance for them.

  • Do not give credit advice. Do not tell a customer that a loan is suitable for them, that they will be approved, or how much they should borrow.
  • Do not discuss their financial position. Income, debts and credit history are between the customer and Propential.
  • Do not complete an application on a customer's behalf or handle their documents.
  • Do not alter any item. Wording, figures, and the line reading “Credit provided by MediPay Holdings Pty Ltd ACL 474336. Subject to lending criteria.” must appear exactly as supplied.
  • Do not crop banners or remove the disclosure to fit a space. Use a smaller size instead.
  • Do not make your quote conditional on the customer using Propential.

The safe sentence: “I can't advise you on finance. Here is the information, and Propential can answer questions directly.”

Getting help

Ask for material rather than making it. If you need a size, a format or a version that is not here, email partners@propential.com.au with what you need and where it will run.

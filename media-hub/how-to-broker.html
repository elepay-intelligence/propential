What this hub is

A fixed set of approved Propential marketing material, prepared for mortgage broker and real estate partners. Everything has been through compliance review. You do not need to submit anything for approval, and you do not need a designer.

There are three kinds of item: client-facing documents you send or forward, an internal reference sheet for your own team, and digital banners for email, your website and social.

What this hub is not. It is not a template library. The wording, figures and the credit disclosure on every item are fixed. If something you need is missing, ask for it rather than making it.

Where to place banners

  • Email signature (600×160). The highest-volume placement you have. Insert as an image, then set its hyperlink to https://propential.com.au. Keep it below your sign-off, above any legal footer.
  • Website footer (1200×250). Sitewide, so it appears on every page a client reads. Link it to https://propential.com.au.
  • Sidebar tower (300×600). Finance, services or blog pages. This one carries a QR code so it still works in a screenshot.
  • Social (1080×1080, 1200×628, 1080×1920). Put the propential.com.au link in the caption or the story link sticker. The QR code covers re-shares and screenshots.

One placement beats five posts. If you only do one thing from this hub, put the signature banner in your email template today.

Rules and compliance

  • Do not alter any item. Wording, figures, and the line reading “Credit provided by MediPay Holdings Pty Ltd ACL 474336. Subject to lending criteria.” must appear exactly as supplied.
  • Do not crop banners or remove the disclosure to fit a space. Use a smaller size instead.
  • Do not add your own claims about rates, approval times, approval likelihood or outcomes.
  • The internal fact sheet is internal. It is written for brokers and is not to be forwarded to clients.
  • Credit assistance stays with you. Where you are providing credit assistance, your own licence obligations, disclosure documents and best-interests duty apply as they normally would.

If in doubt, do not improvise. Email partners@propential.com.au and we will supply an approved version.

Explaining the product in one breath

Propential is a second-mortgage facility from $5,000 to $175,000, secured against residential property, with terms up to 10 years and no early-exit fees. It sits behind the existing first mortgage, which is not disturbed.

The three-step story

  • Bridge. Propential funds the work as a second mortgage. The client's current lender, rate and term stay as they are.
  • Uplift. The renovation, extension or subdivision completes and the property is revalued.
  • Refinance out. You write the refinance at the higher valuation and Propential is repaid at settlement. No early-exit fee for doing it sooner.

Lines that work

  • “We are not touching your current loan.”
  • “This funds the work; we refinance on the finished value.”
  • “There is no penalty for paying it out early.”

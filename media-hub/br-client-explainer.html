STEP ONE

Bridge

Propential funds improvement to your property, secured by caveat (second mortgage in QLD or NT), sitting behind your existing home loan. Your current mortgage remains untouched.

STEP TWO

Uplift

Once the renovation is completed, the property is revalued at what it is now worth, not what it was worth before you started.

STEP THREE

Refinance out

Your broker refinances you at the revalued amount and Propential is repaid from the proceeds. There is no early-exit fee for doing this sooner rather than later.
